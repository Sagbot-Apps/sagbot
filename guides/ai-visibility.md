# AI visibility: what you can fix, and what you can only track

**AI visibility is how present your brand is in AI answers.** Part of it you control directly,
through your own site: whether AI can read it and cite it. The rest (how often you're mentioned,
your share of answers, sentiment) is earned over time and can only be measured. Know which is which
before you spend money on either.

[Check what you control with SagBot](https://sagbot.app/) · [Full guide on sagbot.app](https://sagbot.app/guides/ai-visibility) · [README](../README.md#guides)

## What is AI visibility?

It's how often, and how accurately, AI assistants and answer engines mention, cite and link your
brand when people ask about your field. It depends on two things: whether your site can be read and
trusted, and whether the wider web talks about you.

## What can you fix on your own site?

Whether AI bots are allowed in, whether your text is readable without JavaScript, whether your
structured data says who you are, whether your pages answer questions in quotable passages. These
are properties of your site, so you can check them today and fix them this week.

This is what SagBot checks. **Agent-ready** measures whether bots and agents can reach and use your
site; **Answer-ready** measures whether answer engines are likely to cite it, split into SEO, GEO
and AEO.

| You can fix | You can only track |
|---|---|
| AI bots allowed in `robots.txt` | How often AI answers mention you |
| Text rendered on the server | Your share of answers against competitors |
| Organization data, one consistent name, `sameAs` | Sentiment of those mentions |
| Quotable answer paragraphs and FAQ markup | Which sources an engine picks for a prompt |
| `llms.txt`, Markdown for agents | |

## What can you only track?

How often you're mentioned in answers, your share of answers against competitors, and the sentiment
of those mentions. You can't change these directly; they follow from your site, your coverage and
your reputation over time.

Measuring them takes a different kind of tool: one that runs the same prompts against assistants on
a schedule and records who gets named. SagBot doesn't do that; it checks your site.

## Where should you start?

Fix what you control first. Tracking mentions before your site can be read and cited measures a
ceiling you set yourself. Once your scores are good, tracking tells you whether the rest of your
marketing is moving the numbers.
